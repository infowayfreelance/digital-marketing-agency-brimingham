About Digital Marketing Agency Birmingham

We're a team of Birmingham-based digital marketing specialists who believe great marketing should be measurable, honest and built around the businesses it serves.

Our Story

Born in Birmingham. Built for Birmingham Business.

We started Digital Marketing Agency Birmingham because we saw too many local businesses getting burned by London agencies who didn't understand the West Midlands market, charged too much and delivered too little.

Over the past 10 years, we've grown to a team of specialists — SEO strategists, PPC experts, web developers, designers and content writers — all based right here in Birmingham, all focused on getting you real results.

From a cafe in Moseley to a solicitor's in Edgbaston, a manufacturer in Aston to a tech start-up in Digbeth — we've helped over 200 Birmingham businesses grow their online presence and their revenue. We know the city, we know the competition and we know what works here.
